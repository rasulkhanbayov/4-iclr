E00000. THEORY AUDIT (seventh pass)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
CORRECTNESS. Proposition 2 statement (iii) previously asserted that
the leave-one-theta-out R^2 "has non-positive expectation" and that
the selection rule "returns neither with probability tending to one".

  The first was NOT PROVABLE as written and the second was FALSE as
  written. R^2_LOO is a ratio of dependent random variables; its SIGN
  is random. Simulation at four design sizes puts P(R^2_LOO > 0) at
  5-10% under exactly this model, and the released smoke test contains
  a pure-noise draw scoring +0.035. A bare sign test therefore does
  NOT return "neither" with probability tending to one.

  CORRECTED to what is provable and sufficient:
    R^2_LOO -> 0 in probability, and for any FIXED MARGIN tau > 0 a
    rule accepting only R^2_LOO > tau returns "neither" with
    probability tending to one.
  This makes the margin a load-bearing part of the theorem instead of
  a footnote, which is both more honest and a stronger result.
  Verified numerically: |R^2_LOO| falls 0.026 -> 0.0001 across designs
  and P(R^2_LOO > 0.1) = 0.0000 at every size tested.
  The unproven expectation claim is now recorded in Sec. S3 as an
  explicitly labelled observation, not a result.

COMPLETENESS. The proof asserted "z is drawn independently of the
grid value" as though it were given. It is a DESIGN assumption and was
buried in the proof. It is now stated in Definition 2 itself (the

in Sec. S3: under a confounded design a generator that is entirely

E0. THEORY AND DESIGN PASS (third pass)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The paper repeatedly said the seed-conditional behavior was "a
distinction the current formalism has no parameter for". It now has
one, and the formalism predicts the paper's own negative result.

  - NEW Definition 2 (seed-conditional default). Prior reversion with a
    single global theta_0 is the special case sigma_c^2 = 0, so the new
    model NESTS the existing one rather than competing with it.
  - NEW Proposition 2 (misspecification), proved in supplementary S3:
    under a seed-conditional default, PRI -> 1 and held-out R^2 has
    non-positive expectation, so the selection rule returns "neither"
    WITH PROBABILITY TENDING TO ONE. The paper's awkward negative
    result is now a derived prediction of its own model.
  - NEW Remark (PRI is not identifying): the same signature is produced
    by unstructured noise, so PRI CANNOT establish seed-locking. This
    is a self-correction; the previous draft's headline rested on a
    statistic that does not support it.
  - NEW metric: Seed Determination Index, a one-way random-effects ICC
    over seeds, with FDI as its counterpart for the requested value.
    Verified on synthetic ground truth: SDI = 0.999 seed-locked, 0.000
    faithful, 0.000 pure noise, 0.060 single global default. The raw
    variance share is upward-biased (0.18 where truth is 0.00), so the
    corrected estimator is used.
  - DEFECT FOUND AND FIXED IN YOUR OWN METHOD: select_mechanism
    accepted any POSITIVE held-out R^2. Because that quantity is
    centred on zero when theta is uninformative, its sign is noise, and
    the smoke test reproduces a pure-noise sample being labelled
    "prior_reversion" at R^2 = +0.035. The rule now requires a margin
    (R^2 > 0.1). A test asserts the bare sign test fails where the
    margin rule does not, so the margin cannot be silently removed.
  - Proposition 1 (estimator consistency) was compressed. It is
    standard and is not the paper's novel theory; the novel result now
    carries the section, which is also what the venue expects.
  - EXPERIMENTS.md added: prioritized, executable spec. Tier 0 needs no
    GPU and fits before the deadline.

E. STRATEGIC REFRAME (second pass)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The paper previously led with a NULL ("none honors conditioned
physics") estimated on a survivor subset. That is the claim the
37.5-100% attrition most undermines, and it is also the least novel
part of the work, since Thozhiyoor et al. already showed generators get
gravity wrong. The seed-locked convergence is a POSITIVE, measured,
high-confidence finding that does not depend on attrition at all. The
paper now leads with it.

  - TITLE changed to "The Seed Picks the Physics: Auditing Parameter
    Faithfulness in Frozen Video Generators". The original title is
    preserved as a commented line directly above it in main.tex; swap
    the two lines to revert.
  - ABSTRACT restructured into two paragraphs: instrument, then
    finding. The seed numbers (-11.4 +/- 0.03, -7.1 +/- 0.2) are now
    in the abstract.
  - CONTRIBUTIONS reordered artifact-first, matching how the
    Evaluation & Dataset track is reviewed.
  - The fit-quality gate rate is now a NAMED METRIC (trackability
    rate) reported as a result, not an exclusion criterion. It is
    justified by the VLM decorrelation: a plausibility rater does not
    track it, so it measures something plausibility scores miss.
  - NEW ARGUMENT against the killer objection, in Results: if the
    stimulus were merely un-renderable, surviving values would be
    scattered; instead they are seed-locked with dispersion 0.03-0.13
    and fit R^2 up to 1.000, and replicate under a second tracker.
    Confident well-fit seed-locked motion is what a model IGNORING
    theta produces, not what a model FAILING TO RENDER produces.
  - HEADLINE now stated conditionally: "conditional on producing
    physically trackable motion, none of the three models detectably
    honors a text-specified physical parameter." Trackability bounds
    the coverage of that claim, not its validity.
  - CONCLUSION adds the practical implication: a benchmark that
    averages over seeds reports a smooth plausible mean and hides that
    each sample is locked to a wrong constant, so seed-resolved
    reporting is not optional.
